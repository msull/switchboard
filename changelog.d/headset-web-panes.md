Added: on the headset, a web page can sit in either side pane of a card, on a board as a tile, or in a window of its own: the system's web view, with back, forward and reload, so a page a session serves on the network renders as it does in Safari. A typed address or a page opened before is chosen in a pane's chooser, a web link tapped in a card asks where to open it (left pane, right pane, its own window or Safari), a link dragged from another app drops onto a pane or a board, and a page is pinned to a board from its bar or the board's Add page.
