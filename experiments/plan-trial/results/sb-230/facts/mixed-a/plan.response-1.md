1. **The `GET` exemption.** Agreed, and the plan's sentence was wrong. Changed: "The signals", decision 2, the Origin-rule bullet. `DeviceSeen` is now skipped for a cookie-authenticated `GET` with no `Origin`, so a foreign same-site page cannot forge "seen" or trigger a `devices.json` write. The pool and inbox effects are stated as accepted, and the §5 amendment in "Docs" now says so. Also changed: "Actions and effects" (`authenticate`) and "The tests" (a new `last_seen` test: a cookie `GET` leaves it alone; a bearer `GET` and a cookie `POST` with the right `Origin` move it). The cost is that a page in poll mode shows the device as seen only at its last send or socket open. The plan says that.

2. **Where the cookie goes.** Agreed. Changed: decision 2 has a new bullet, "Where the cookie goes", covering the token reaching other https servers on the address under `/api/v1`, the same-name overwrite possible because `server_id` is public in `/info`, and the fallback for an owner who rejects it. The `docs/remote-client.md` §5 line 312 amendment in "Docs" now says it, and the dialog sentence in "The UI" carries it.

3. **Cookie lifetime.** Agreed on both counts. Changed: decision 2 now says the 400 days is a choice (Chrome's cap, no support in the facts file), and `GET /device` re-sends the cookie so a page in use slides. Protocol step 4 and departure 3 use the wording "not paired, or revoked on the Mac, or this browser dropped the pairing". "Docs" notes the stale device row in the design.md status paragraph. The tests add that `GET /device` re-sends the cookie.

4. **`web: true` type change.** Agreed. Changed: the field is now the string `credential: "cookie"`, read with the existing `field()` helper. `"token"` is reserved for the fallback, which then adds a value only. Updated in decision 2, protocol step 2, "Actions and effects", "The tests", the order of work, and "Contract additions". The `Listen` `web: bool` in "The record" is a separate possible owner switch and is unchanged.

5. **Absent `sb.host`.** Agreed. Changed: protocol step 3 and "The record". With no `sb.host` the page skips the identity check and goes to `/device`, because the cookie may still be valid. The plan says the check is lost after a week idle and is accepted, because it only guards against a wrong genuine host. §5 in "Docs" says it too.

6. **Commit message.** Agreed. Changed: "Commit message" now says the body lists the contract additions, and that it ends with no attribution trailer whatever a harness reminder says. "Contract additions" gained the `credential` field, the sliding cookie and the `DeviceSeen` rule.
