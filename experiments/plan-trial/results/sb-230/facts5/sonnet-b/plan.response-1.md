# Response to plan.feedback-1

1. **Click-through is an untested premise; spike 23 only gates on the cookie.** Agreed, and changed. "The tests" (spike 23 paragraph) now names two stop results in order: no click-through offered on a real iPhone/iPad Safari or current Chrome to the IP-SAN certificate, then the cookie not stored or sent. On either the implementer reports before writing anything else. Decision 1 states the click-through is Unresolved, cites the contradictory vendor text from the review, and says the owner then picks the certificate download. "Follow-ups for the owner" says the download is what the owner picks on that stop. The download itself is still not built: it is a second pre-pairing path and a decision of its own.

2. **Code in the query where a fragment would do.** Agreed, and changed. Decision 3, `invite_url` (The actions and effects) and `app.js` (The UI) now use `/#code=...&key=...&id=...` and `location.hash`. The script still calls `replaceState` so the fragment leaves the address bar and history. Decision 3 gives the reason and the "Where this departs" section has a new bullet for it. I kept `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` in decision 4 as a one-header defence and reworded it, since the fragment never reaches a `Referer`. The Swift `parse` and the shared literal in the tests follow the new form because they are described as the same literal as `invite_url`.

3. **The cookie changes `docs/remote-client.md:312` without citing it.** Agreed, and changed. "Where this departs" has a new bullet quoting line 312 and saying both halves, the bearer header and the pinned connection, are given up for a browser only. It is amended in place, native clients keep the rule, and the token is still never in a URL. "Docs and order" now says line 312's sentence is amended in place rather than a paragraph appended.

4. **Reissue premise: rebind trigger.** Agreed, and changed. In "The actions and effects" (Certificate) the first bind computes the SAN and nothing else. A rebind happens only if an `OpenPairing` finds `local_address()` different from `remote_cert_ip`, and `refresh_remote_certificate()` returns when they are equal. The text says a rebind drops every paired socket (`bind_remote`, `src/app.rs:2936-2990`), that this happens once per address change and not once per window, and that cookie-paired devices keep working after an address change while a fresh QR needs the rebind to get the new address into the SAN.
