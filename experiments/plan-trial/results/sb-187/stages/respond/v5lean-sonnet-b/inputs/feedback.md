1. Step 4 (`start_workflow`) assumes the failed review's copy was made from the plan that is still current, and nothing supports that. The plan's only evidence is `review_subject` at `scheduler.rs:10534-10548`, which picks the newest *Complete* `plan` attempt. The plan's rule picks `latest_attempt(t, &stage.name, ctx)` (`scheduler.rs:9444`) and then its `plan.md`. Between a failed review/1 and the owner's `rerun` answer, the plan stage can be redone (a send-back or restart produces a newer Complete plan/2). `review_subject` would then copy plan/2. The plan's rule would copy review/1's revision of plan/1 over it, and `seeded_from` would record that as intended. Neither the Facts nor the tree say this cannot happen. Fix: carry the failed copy only if the `Complete` attempt `review_subject` would choose is the one the failed attempt was copied from (compare against the plan attempt that was current when the failed review started, or against plan/N by number). Otherwise fall back to the original and say so in the question. Name the check in the plan, and add a test for a plan redone between the failure and the rerun.
